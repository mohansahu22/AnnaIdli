"We believe great South Indian cooking begins with utmost reverence for patience and pure ingredients."

At Anna Idli, our kitchen follows time-honored preparation traditions: stone-grinding whole urad dal and parboiled rice, allowing the batter to naturally ferment over hours until light and aerated.

From pure aromatic ghee glistening over golden roast dosas to the freshly pounded coriander and red chili in our sambar, every meal is prepared to bring genuine culinary hospitality from the Southern states directly to Shankar Nagar, Raipur.
